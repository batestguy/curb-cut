The figures use the Washington Group's disability cut-off: "a lot of difficulty" or "cannot do at all" in at least one of six everyday activities (seeing, hearing, walking or climbing steps, remembering or concentrating, self-care, and communicating). Every estimate here comes with its 95% confidence interval and a precision flag. The NDHS 2024 report already publishes state figures for each sex; this site adds both sexes together with intervals and accessible, downloadable tables. The state figures for each activity at the cut-off are published as downloadable data; state pages come in a later phase. How the figures were made.

      What was assumed: JONAPWD's 2025 estimates

      These are estimates built on an assumption

      No one was counted. JONAPWD applied the global 15% disability rate to 2025 population projections. Totals may differ from JONAPWD's by a few people (rounding). Because every state gets the same rate, no map or spatial analysis is made of these figures.

      Explore by state

      Choose a state, then press Show figures to see a short profile of the state with its measured disability figure, a map showing where it is, and JONAPWD's assumed figures by type of disability. The state is also outlined on the map and highlighted in the chart of JONAPWD's figures above. JONAPWD's figures are queried in your browser from a Parquet file with DuckDB-WASM; nothing is sent to a server. The first query can take a few seconds while the in-browser database loads.
