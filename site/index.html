These are estimates built on an assumption

Explore by state

Choose a state, then press Show figures to see a short profile of the state, a map showing where it is, and its figures by type of disability. The state is also highlighted in the chart of all states above. The figures are queried in your browser from a Parquet file with DuckDB-WASM; nothing is sent to a server. The first query can take a few seconds while the in-browser database loads.
