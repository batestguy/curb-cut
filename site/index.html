How many Nigerians have each type of disability? JONAPWD's 2025 estimates

These are estimates built on an assumption

JONAPWD did not count or survey anyone for these figures. It applied the global benchmark that 15% of any population has a disability to the National Population Commission's 2025 population projections, then divided the result by type of disability. Read them as JONAPWD's assumption-based estimates, not as measured prevalence.

Totals on this page are sums of JONAPWD's published state figures. They can differ from JONAPWD's own printed totals by a few persons, likely because of rounding.
