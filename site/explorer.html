Data explorer: disability in the DHS 2024 survey

Every DHS 2024 disability figure the site publishes, for each state, each geopolitical zone and Nigeria as a whole: choose what to show, then press Show figures. The table has each figure's 95% confidence interval, its precision flag and the number of respondents behind it, and you can sort it by any column. Figures for fewer than 30 respondents are never shown. The data are queried in your browser with DuckDB-WASM; nothing is sent to a server. What the precision flags mean.

Choose what to show

  • The 37 state pages: every DHS 2024 figure for each state, with a CSV and a JSON download under each table.
  • The data files the explorer reads, dhs2024_state_prevalence.parquet and dhs2024_national_zone.parquet (Parquet), described column by column in the data dictionary.

Sources

The DHS Program, Nigeria Demographic and Health Survey 2024 (National Population Commission and ICF), Washington Group Short Set; estimates by Curb Cut from the survey microdata, used for the registered project "Curb Cut: disability prevalence and poverty in Nigeria". Only disclosure-checked aggregates are published. Reuse is subject to the source terms; cite the sources.

If anything is hard to use or looks wrong, email batesthommie@gmail.com (backup: jzbannister@atbu.edu.ng); no account is needed. With a GitHub account you can also report a problem on GitHub.
