An assumed rate against a survey measure, state by state

JONAPWD's 2025 state figures apply one assumed disability rate to every state's projected population. Here you can change that rate and see, for each state, whether it falls above, inside or below the DHS 2024 survey's 95% confidence interval. The assumed rate is an assumption; the survey measures one stated definition, for one age group.

Sources

The DHS Program, Nigeria Demographic and Health Survey 2024 (National Population Commission and ICF), Washington Group Short Set; estimates by Curb Cut from the survey microdata, used for the registered project "Curb Cut: disability prevalence and poverty in Nigeria". Only disclosure-checked aggregates are published. Population: National Bureau of Statistics, Demographic Bulletin 2023, Annex 3 (source: National Population Commission). The assumed rate: JONAPWD, 2025 Assumptive Disability Disaggregated Data, cited, not redistributed. Reuse is subject to the source terms; cite the sources.
