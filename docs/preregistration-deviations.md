# P5 pre-registration: deviations log

This is the log that section 10 of `docs/preregistration.md` ("Deviations") requires: any change after registration (OSF https://osf.io/kes3p/, 2026-11-01) is recorded here with its date, reason and effect, and the registered analysis is reported alongside the changed one wherever possible.

Each deviation is a heading of the form `## YYYY-MM-DD: what changed`. `curbcut_ingest.restricted.p5_publish` counts those headings and publishes the count (or "none") in `data/public/aggregates/p5_mpi_summary.csv`, which the poverty page shows.

No deviations as of 2026-11-01.
